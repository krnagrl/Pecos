Short-Term Credit Facilities

Pecos provides short-term credit facilities to businesses and individuals requiring temporary working capital or short-duration funding. Our objective is to provide fast, transparent and flexible financing solutions for short-term financial needs.

Credit Terms

• Fixed Fee: 1.10% per month
• Credit Duration: 1 to 6 Months
• Available for: Businesses & Individuals
• Renewal: Can be renewed upon approval before expiry

Suitable For

  • Working capital requirements
  • Business expansion opportunities
  • Inventory purchases
  • Short-term cash flow management
  • Emergency financial requirements
  • Bridge financing

Key Benefits

  • Simple and transparent pricing
  • No hidden monthly charges
  • Flexible repayment periods
  • Quick processing and approval
  • Renewal option available at maturity, subject to review
Fee Structure

A fixed fee of 1.10% per month is applicable on the sanctioned credit amount for the approved duration. Credit facilities may be renewed before the expiry date after review and approval.
