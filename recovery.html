What We Do

Pecos provides money recovery services for clients who have outstanding payments from their customers, buyers, borrowers or other parties.

If someone has a genuine payment due to you and you need assistance in following up with them, you can appoint Pecos to handle the recovery process on your behalf.

Our Services

Unpaid Bills & Invoices

Customer Dues

Assistance in recovering money that customers have not paid within the agreed time.

Business Payments

Recovery assistance for outstanding amounts payable between businesses and their customers.
