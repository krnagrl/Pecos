About Pecos

Pecos is a diversified business enterprise focused primarily on finance, credit and investment-related activities, with additional interests in property, recovery and other complementary sectors.

This website provides an overview of the business interests and activities associated with Pecos. It is intended as a corporate information website and does not constitute an offer or solicitation for services.

Finance & Credit

Pecos has interests in finance and credit-related activities, including lending, credit facilitation and related financial business activities.

Investment Management

Investment activities covering securities, mutual funds and other permitted investment opportunities, with a focus on long-term capital allocation.

Debt Recovery

Interest in receivables management, payment follow-up and debt recovery activities undertaken in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

Real Estate

Property-related investments and activities including acquisition, leasing, brokerage and development of income-generating properties.

Forex

Interest in foreign-exchange related business activities and opportunities, undertaken through appropriate authorised arrangements where required.

Facility Management

Business interests involving the management and maintenance of commercial and other properties and their associated facilities.

Defence Services

Interest in defence-sector supply, support, logistics and related business opportunities, subject to applicable government requirements.

Hospitality & Leisure

Hospitality and leisure interests including Club Pecos and related recreational and entertainment activities.

Our Approach

Pecos focuses on building businesses with long-term potential rather than pursuing short-term opportunities.

We look for businesses where disciplined capital allocation, practical operations and long-term relationships can create sustainable value.

Diversification

Our interests span several sectors. Finance and investments form the core of our business interests, while property, recovery, facilities and other activities provide additional diversification.

Long-Term Vision

The objective is to gradually develop a diversified portfolio of businesses and assets capable of generating sustainable income and long-term capital value.

Business Model

Pecos combines operating businesses with investment and asset-based interests. Depending on the activity, the business may operate independently, invest directly, work with business partners or participate through appropriately authorised entities.

The overall objective is diversification of income, disciplined deployment of capital and gradual development of long-term business assets.
