Welcome to AllBold Farms

AllBold Farms is an agricultural venture based in Babhulgaon, Akola, Maharashtra. Our farms focus on fruit cultivation and cotton plantations, with produce prepared for both domestic markets and export opportunities.

We aim to combine traditional farming knowledge with disciplined cultivation, harvesting and supply practices.

From Farm to Market

  • 1. Cultivate and maintain our crops
  • 2. Harvest at suitable maturity
  • 3. Sort and prepare produce for supply
  • 4. Serve domestic and export markets

Domestic & Export Supply

Our agricultural produce is intended for supply beyond the farm, including domestic trade and export markets. We focus on consistent cultivation and careful handling so that suitable produce can move efficiently from Babhulgaon to buyers and markets.

Fruit Cultivation

Chikoo, papaya, guava and sitaphal form the fruit portfolio of AllBold Farms. Our objective is to maintain healthy plantations and provide quality seasonal produce to the market.

Cotton Plantation

Cotton is an important part of our agricultural activity. Production is focused on cultivation and supply of cotton from the Akola region to appropriate buyers and markets.
