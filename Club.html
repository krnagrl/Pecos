Welcome to Club Pecos

Club Pecos brings together accommodation, recreation, wellness, dining and outdoor spaces in one relaxed destination. Whether you are staying overnight, spending an afternoon with friends or enjoying a quiet weekend, the club is designed around simple leisure and comfort.

Club Pecos Facilities

Rooms & Stay

Comfortable rooms for short stays, weekends and private club visits.

Game Room

Table tennis, carrom, chess, billiards, cards and other indoor recreation.

Spa & Wellness

A quiet space for relaxation, wellness treatments and personal downtime.

Bar & Restaurant

A social dining area serving meals, refreshments and beverages, subject to applicable laws and licences.

Swimming Pool

A dedicated pool area for leisure, recreation and relaxed afternoons.

Park & Gardens

Open green areas for walking, sitting, conversation and spending time outdoors.

Outdoor Leisure
