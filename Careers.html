Careers at Pecos

At Pecos, we believe that every successful career begins with curiosity, discipline, and continuous learning. We welcome passionate individuals who are eager to explore the worlds of finance, investments, real estate, and legal services.

Our Philosophy

We are here to gain knowledge and experience by sharing it with each other. Every internship at Pecos is designed to provide practical exposure, professional guidance, and opportunities to learn through real business operations.

Current Internship Opportunities

Stock Market Intern

Allowance: Minimum ₹5,000 per month

Real Estate Intern

Eligibility: Male / Female

Allowance: Minimum ₹5,000 per month

Law Intern

Eligibility: Male / Female
